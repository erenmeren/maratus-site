How it works for your customer

  1. The screen lights up

    Your till sends a link, and the code for it appears in front of the customer.

  2. They point a phone at it

    The phone’s own camera reads the code. Nothing to install, nothing to type.

  3. Your page opens

    It’s your own link, on your own site. Maratus doesn’t host the page and doesn’t see what they open.

The cables stay with you

Power comes in over USB-C at the back of the Dock. The customer’s side stays clear.

The screen lifts off its Dock

Off the pins, then straight up. The Dock stays where it is.

USB-C is underneath

On the Dock it isn’t needed. Off the Dock, this is where the power goes in.

Twenty gold contacts, flush with the case

The back of the screen is one flat plane.

Twenty spring pins on the Dock

The Dock’s USB-C power goes up through them when the screen sits down again.

It works off the Dock too

  1. The magnets are in its back

    Two discs, hidden just under the case, one each side.

  2. It snaps onto steel

    Bring its back close and it holds. Power goes in underneath, over USB-C.

  3. It sits in a holder

    The kind of security holder shops already use.

  4. And back on its Dock

    By the till, powered again through the pins.

Steel post, holder and cable are examples.
